# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

mikrokhoros contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to making project
spaces respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, ability, appearance, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about evidence and impact.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Follow maintainer requests intended to keep a discussion productive and safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and retaliation for good-faith
reports are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through the hosting
platform's private moderation or reporting channel. Do not use the public issue
tracker when doing so would expose a target or reporter. Maintainers will protect
privacy as far as practical, evaluate reports fairly, and may edit or remove content,
issue warnings, limit participation, or permanently exclude participants.

Maintainers must recuse themselves from handling a report when they are directly
involved. Platform-wide abuse may also be reported to the repository host.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project chat, events, and public representation of mikrokhoros.
